## Required files

- PowerPoint: maximum 5 slides; selected theme is explicit; implementation design and key learning technologies are clear.
- Video: 5–7 minutes; MP4; project interactions and evaluation-relevant features are visible.
- Cloud links: test in a private/incognito window without signing in.
- Team list: 1–5 final members; cannot be changed after submission.
- Source/platform link: include the final repository or project page in the PowerPoint.

## Judge-facing QA

- Simulation-based learning (25%): show the complete learning loop and at least one safety gate changing behavior.
- Creativity and innovation (20%): show the integrated state-aware procedure and synchronized hologram output.
- Educational impact (20%): state the learning outcomes and the planned evaluation measures; do not invent study results.
- UI/UX and engagement (15%): make the instruction panel, spatial cue, hand interaction, and feedback readable.
- AI/gamification/adaptive learning (10%): describe the implemented feature accurately as rules-based state-adaptive guidance.
- Sustainability/accessibility/ethics (10%): show multimodal cues, adjustable speed, safe virtual rehearsal, and practice without physical consumables.

- Deck contains exactly 5 slides, no unresolved placeholders, and working links.
- Video duration is between 5:00 and 7:00 and plays from start to finish after upload.
- Voice-over is intelligible on laptop speakers; captions match the final narration.
- Lower-thirds use each person's full name and correct role; professor and Yilin placeholders have been replaced.
- No third-party media appears without permission or attribution.
